Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026.

This integration is used to manage and review the authorized Yuna Carter TikTok account.

Data accessed

With the account owner's authorization, the integration may access TikTok profile information, public-post information, follower and following counts, like counts, and video/post counts.

Use of data

Authorized data is used only for Yuna Carter account operations, internal reporting, content-status checks, and related automation.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials and tokens, when issued, are stored only in the authorized private local automation environment. They are not published on this site or intentionally included in public reports.

Sharing

Authorized TikTok data is not sold and is not shared with unrelated third parties.

Retention and deletion

Authorization data may be removed when the integration is disconnected or no longer required.
